# ToledoLokal — Business Owner Guide

A step-by-step guide for getting your business onto ToledoLokal and adding a
manager to help run your page.

---

## Part 1 — How to Sign Up Your Business

### Step 1 — Open the sign-up page
Go to the app and tap **Sign Up** (the `/auth` page). If you already see the
login form, tap **"Don't have an account? Sign up"** at the bottom.

### Step 2 — Choose "Business"
Under **"I am a…"**, select **🏢 Business** (or **🚚 Food Truck** if that's
you). Don't pick "Toledo Explorer."

### Step 3 — Enter your details
- **Name** — your name
- **Email** — use your **business email** if you can (personal emails like
  Gmail require admin approval before going live, which is slower)
- **Password** — must meet the checklist shown (8+ characters, an uppercase
  letter, a lowercase letter, a number, and a symbol)

Then tap **Create Account**.

### Step 4 — Confirm your email
You'll see a **"Check your email"** screen. Open the email we send and tap the
**confirmation link** inside it.
- Can't find it? Check your spam/junk folder.
- Still nothing? Tap **"Didn't get the link? Resend."**

### Step 5 — Land on the business form
Clicking the link signs you in and takes you straight to **Create Business**
(`/create-business`).

### Step 6 — Fill in your business listing
**Required:**
- **Business Name**
- **Category**
- **Neighborhood**
- **Description** — a short blurb about your business

**Optional (recommended):**
- **Address**, **Phone**, **Website**
- **Social links** — Instagram, TikTok, Facebook
- **"How did you hear about Toledo Lokal?"**

### Step 7 — Submit
Tap **Submit for Approval**. Your listing is reviewed before it goes live
(faster if you used a business email).

### Step 8 — You're in
Once approved, your business is live on ToledoLokal and you can manage
everything from your **Dashboard** (edit details, add staff/managers, post
deals, etc.).

> **Note:** Some businesses get a guided **5-step onboarding wizard**
> (Basics → Location → Photos → Hours → Done) instead of the single form, but
> the "Submit for Approval" form above is the main path. There is **no 6-digit
> code** to type — you just click the link in your email.

## Part 2 — How to Add a Manager to Your Business Page

*(Do this after your business is approved and you're signed in as the owner.)*

### Step 1 — Go to your Dashboard
From the app, open your **Dashboard**.

### Step 2 — Open Staff & Scanners
Go to **Staff & Scanners** (`/dashboard/staff`). This is where you manage who
can help run your page.

### Step 3 — Tap "Invite Staff"
In the top right, tap **Invite Staff**. A dialog titled **"Invite Staff
Member"** opens.

### Step 4 — Enter their info
- **Email Address** — the email of the person you want to add
- **Role** — choose **Manager**
  - *Manager* = extra permissions to help manage the page
  - *Staff (Scanner only)* = can only scan customer QR codes

### Step 5 — Send the invite
Tap **Send Invitation**. You'll get a confirmation, and they'll receive an
invite email.
- If the email doesn't send for any reason, you'll get a **Copy Link** button
  so you can share the invite manually.

### Step 6 — Track the invite
On the same page you'll now see:
- **Pending Invitations** — shows their email, role, and when the invite
  expires (invites last **7 days**); you can **Copy Link** or **Delete** it here.
- **Current Staff** — they'll appear here once they accept.

### Step 7 — The manager accepts (their side)
1. They open the invite email and tap **Accept Invitation** (lands on
   `/accept-invitation`).
2. If they're not signed in, they tap **Sign In to Accept** and create an
   account (or log in) first.
3. Once signed in, they tap **Accept Invitation** and see a **"You're In!"**
   confirmation.

### Step 8 — Done
They now appear under **Current Staff** as a **Manager** and can help manage
the page. You can remove anyone anytime with the **Delete** button next to
their name.

> **Notes:**
> - Only the **business owner** can invite, change, or remove staff.
> - The invite is **tied to the email you enter** — the manager should accept
>   using that same email address.
